# Mel-scale
##

- for anything less than 700 we would be calculating log between 1.5 to 2 which has a higher gradient than after 700 (numbers would be closer to each other) showing how we are more sensitive to numbers below 700
- the difference between 100 mels would be the same pitch difference to the human year but the difference felt by 100Hz is dependent on whether the frequency is low or high (100 to 200 is more noticeable than 10000 to 10100)
	- If two pairs of sounds are separated by 500 mels, a human listener will say the "jump" in pitch feels the same for both pairs, regardless of where they are on the frequency spectrum.
