# pragmatics
##
- this is like semantics but you also consider the context around it? 

> the study of meaning in contexts

- While **semantics** deals with the literal, dictionary meaning of words, **pragmatics** is what requires us to understand the surrounding context to grasp the speaker's true intent
- To illustrate this, the sources provide a classic example: if someone asks you, "Do you know what time it is?", it is **inappropriate to simply answer "yes"**. While "yes" perfectly answers the literal, grammatical question being asked, the _pragmatic_ meaning of the question in almost all contexts is an implicit request to be told the current time.
