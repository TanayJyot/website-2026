# third-variable problem
- There is something else that is causing both of the variables making the two variables correlated.
- Third variables can be responsible for the correlation that we observe in two other variables. In our example of cyberbullying and video game preferences, having antisocial tendencies could be a third variable that predicts both a choice of violent games and a tendency to engage in cyberbullying. The possibility of third variables is one reason that we must be careful when we reach conclusions based on correlational data.
