- A **diphthong** is a "complex" vowel where your mouth actually changes shape while you are saying it.
- **Monophthong:** The mouth stays in one position (e.g., "bit" [ih]).
- **Diphthong:** The articulators (tongue, lips, jaw) **move** from one vowel position toward another within the same syllable.
