# Research Lead @ Lee Language Lab
*Mar 2025 – Jan 2026 · DCS Research Award*

Hokkien has 30M+ speakers but few accessible learning or translation tools. I led a student team building a Hokkien language-learning app and running user studies on it.

- Implemented learning features including spaced repetition (FSRS), streaks and badges.
- Ran user studies on the app's features and worked on the paper write-up.
- Interviewed and onboarded new CSC494/495 students for the lab.
