Whether ‘you have a life’ isn’t determined by the money in your bank account or likes in your social media post…

Having a life is determined by your neutral state. How you feel from within while doing anything and nothing. When there is nothing stimulating you. When you are in control.

This is the quality of your life. To measure it, lock the door of your room and do nothing and observe how it feels.

There will be multiple thoughts bumping one after the other. The thoughts that affect your mood at all times while overstimulation besieges them.

How you feel after a while will portray your quality of life. Once you learn more about yourself through analysing your thoughts in this hour, you can improve your quality.

You will then be able to differentiate between what really matters and what doesn’t and which thoughts and feelings are worth having in your mind. You will be in the 4th state where you watch your thoughts and control them instead of engaging with them.
