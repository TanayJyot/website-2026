title: "You may be fooled"
date: "2024-11"

---

You are more likely to be listening to a fool than an intellect.

Because fools are extremely confident while the smart ones are not.

Because the smart ones need to break what they believe in order to become who they are. Their confidence also breaks with their beliefs…

The different ideas that the smart ones know make them less confident because something might come up tomorrow and they may be wrong.

However, for society the smart ones must rise to save us from being misguided.

To do this, be very confident that you can do something but do realise that you may not know the exact path to get there. So be willing to learn more and more so that you are closer to the truth.
