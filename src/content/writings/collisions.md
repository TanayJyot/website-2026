To be innovative you need to borrow from multiple disciplines. Obsession is important but it is equally important to do multiple things and find ways to connect each thing you do to the other. Work with people that are high energy, high integrity and highly intelligent in their niche whatever that niche may be. Whenever you notice someone may have a different mindset than you, share your ideas and plans with them. Innovation comes from collisions not encouragement. Surrounding yourself with disagreeable people is more useful than agreeable people (Think Again). This is how strength of weak ties. They think differently.
