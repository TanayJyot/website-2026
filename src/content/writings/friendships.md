If you do not have a lot of friends, it may be because of the kind of thoughts you have.

Most of mediocre society loves conversations about other people or else they would talk about the news. The people who are into the news will avoid the talks about other people and vice versa.

If you talk about things more puny than people or more intellectual than the news, you will find yourself in a tough spot. Because people become friends with people who they can relate to.

If popularity was the goal, then the solution would be to simply change the room. Talk to people who are like you, but if that isn’t your goal then read further.

Because if you really wanna improve, people who are like you won’t push you, they would make you comfortable. People who are unlike you will challenge you. The person of higher intellect is also hypothetical and needs the guy with the news to bring him to practicality.

So expose yourself to people you would hate to be around because your thinking may reveal a different side of them. That’s how one can grow.

The goal isn’t to have friends that say ‘yes’ to everything you say. It is to have people that you can rely on to tell you exactly when you are going wrong because of their completely different life experiences.
