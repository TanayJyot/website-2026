title: "The wrongdoing of another"
date: "2023-07"
label: "July 2023"
section: "short"
---

When someone does something wrong we tell ourselves that they made this mistake…

But then why do we assume that when we do something wrong circumstances weren’t in our favour…

We tend to fit people in boxes based on their past actions, we want to feel a certain way about them the moment we meet them and we try to judge our way out of it…

But maybe you are the one that can help them change, you are the person who can turn their life around by just one little suggestion.

Why would you be the only one to deserve a chance? Is that justified?
