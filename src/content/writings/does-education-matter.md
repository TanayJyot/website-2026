## What am I even doing?

When I was preparing for my board examinations. It suddenly hit me, what am I even doing. What I am rote learning right now is gonna be useless to me in the future and I will probably forget about it. So why am I even spending so much time on something that is rather temporary?

One solution to this problem that currently comes up to me is that maybe that is what *needed* then. For doing good that time as different times require different things as a solution.

Another thing is that maybe due to the position I had put myself into, I had to rely on a tool because that was my only option and in a longer time scale I could have resorted to something else.

It also makes me wonder why does Education even matter and why is there so much eminence on someone who is better educated? What I have seen is soft skills matter a lot. What you develop of them matters a lot and you can hire someone more educated than you if you have got good soft skills and have the ability to think logically but maybe I think so because I failed for once from getting to a position where I needed to and so I need some kind of justification to ensure that I have something. Something to say to people that will laugh at me. But again in a longer time scale this won’t matter. In the longer timescale the idea I learn will matter though. The way I structure my thoughts due to the education will matter and that is maybe why getting a good education itself will matter because a good education develops someone’s thinking ability and I don’t refer to specialisation here, not degrees and all of that. But 12th grade education. The combinators within that kind of education are maybe to ensure everyone gets somewhere but for people who are trying to change the world or something of a similar endeavour. They generally seem to realise and decide to do 10 other things apart from this education. They know what will matter to them and how much of it will and they tend to know what they want so they can choose what to focus on and say no to everything else. This is a skill I need to develop and I am working on. Understanding thyself thoroughly.

## To answer the question

And to answer the question, yes it matters. Doing well in traditional education matters because it guarantees you something in the short run. But then it becomes a matter of recognising, what you need to absorb for the future and what you need not. Because it gives you ‘some’ path. But I think we can now choose our path according to what we want. I have been granted that freedom and I might as well take it.

## The biggest mistake I made

The biggest mistake I made around that time was that even though I observed things weren’t going that well I decided to ignore them. It’s not like the universe didn’t show me the signs of what was happening. I saw the pattern and still decided to ignore it. I say that I did a ton of other things around that time because of which I couldn’t do well in education. But I beg to question myself, how much did I study after I was done with my school hours? Not a lot and I knew I needed to do it. I just chose not to and there is really no one to blame for that. It is as if I was escaping education by doing 10s of other things even though I knew this was important. What I need to ensure now is I do not repeat this same mistake in college and I find a way to get everything and excel at it, because I am Tanay and I can pretty much do it. I just need to change the decisions I am making and I am moving towards it. The top time consumers that I had around that time were Valorant and the gym and now I am realising how much time I was actually supposed to spend on those. Heck I didn’t even go judiciously to the gym. There was this weird leniency within me throughout that entire year and ego had somewhat overtaken me. I didn’t realise what was essential and what wasn’t even though I knew everything. I knew my duties and I knew what was to be done. I was just lenient and thought I deserved a break because I pulled off something inhuman when I didn’t really do so.

There was a purpose behind the way things messed up. The purpose was for me to learn from those things. The choices will be even more relevant and what happened now was to ensure that I was ready to face that with all my might and now I will. Change starts now.
