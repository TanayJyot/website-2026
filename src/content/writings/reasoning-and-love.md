In the realm of love, reasoning ceases to exist…

Because similar to reasoning, love is a force in itself and when you decide to reason love dies out. It’s duality all over again.

You tend to forget what love is all about, it’s about being there for each other regardless of reason. It’s about deciding who you wish to care the most about…

Instead of contemplating what you want love to be like according to your ‘reasons’, try to accentuate what you want love to ‘feel’ like.

What are the innate values that you want in the other person, because everything else can be adjusted.

There are no two ‘perfect’ beings, because as humans we are flawed and certain flaws never fixate themselves.

Choosing the person who ticks the boxes rather than the person who understands the eminence of values is impressive and popular but do you want the person that society loves or who you love?

You will have to adjust, it is only a matter of adjusting less or more, you will be disappointed but will you realise the beauty of imperfectness or the reckoning of disapproval.

Will you capitalise or miss out on the opportunity to grow together?
