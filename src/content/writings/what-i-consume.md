I have also seemed to develop this weird habit of having a third person to talk to. Because I have gotten too used to it and I am constantly trying to impress this person. When there is nothing to impress. I am thinking about things that contribute to nothing in my life because I am consuming too much information, even though I don’t acknowledge it at best. I have been browsing mindlessly. This itself contributes to most of the things I think about.

- Thoughts work on the basis of the information I consume, if I can control what I consume, I can control where my thoughts hover and I can get closer to figuring it out.

- Is it that simple? To some extent, yes? But there are a lot of other controllable factors to look into.
