---
title: "Battling every day is a choice"
date: "2023-06-05"
label: "June 5, 2023"
section: "journal"
---

Battling every day is a choice we make. We can have completely pleasant, so called “nice” lives but battling doesn’t give us that. That is the cost of the battle. We think we are forced to battle, but really we are not.

You can choose mediocrity at any given time, but can you consciously choose not to be mediocre? That is the question. Hard things will require hard decisions, it isn’t appealing because of the easiness that comes later but for the responsibility that comes with it. I need to remind myself of that.

It is impossible for every situation to give you immediate outputs. We need to expand the timescale to get those outputs. Sometimes, the ripple effects will give you those outputs, because even though you went for a different purpose you got a completely different but better outcome out of it.
