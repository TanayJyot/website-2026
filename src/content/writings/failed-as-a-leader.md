During the SMC155 seminar I failed as a leader because I failed to recognise that maybe I was biased and maybe I was missing out on something. I was too overconfident that even when all the signs were against me I was afraid to think again. Because of which I could have lost but fate being who she is saved me. Doesn’t mean I was right, I was just lucky and I don’t rely on chance.
