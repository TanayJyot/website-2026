title: "Working on yourself"
date: "2023-05"
label: "May 2023"
section: "short"
---

You can’t help other people if they do not want to be helped.

You cannot just get better by hiring the best coaches, finding the best playground, getting the best teachers.

Unless you choose to do a little extra, if you are willing to change. As willingness needs to be from within. To know that you can be so much more than what you currently are.

So instead of telling people or yourself to be better. First find reasons to be better and then help others find their reasons.

Because when you have a reason, there will be no need for motivation.
