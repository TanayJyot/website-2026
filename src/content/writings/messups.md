To recover from any mess up, realise the mistakes you made.

Maybe there were situations beyond your control but there is always something you could have done differently, realise that something and make it a part of your life.

Because when you take accountability, things are now under your control and you can change the situation accordingly to suit you.

Giving up and owning up are equally difficult
