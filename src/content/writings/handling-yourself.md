title: "Handling yourself"
date: "2023-06"
label: "June 2023"
section: "short"
---

No one is responsible for listening to your problems.

You owe yourself to listen to your problems, because you care about yourself.

It is difficult but it is also a superpower. You decide who you are because you can create reasons to do whatever you want.

You can’t lose when you can separate yourself from your thoughts and deal with them, you can then understand who you are vs who you think you are.
