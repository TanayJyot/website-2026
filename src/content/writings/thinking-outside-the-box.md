**The 4 types of thinking I can think of**:
1. Thinking within the constraints of society. Thinking in the box.
2. Thinking outside the box. Knowing the constraints but looking at something else. This might be close to what the box is because you are still inclined towards it.
3. Ignoring the box entirely. This can lead you to deliberately find something very distinct which can be disadvantageous.
4. Not even knowing there is a box. You do your own thing because you don’t know what society expects, so you are free in a sense so this should work in theory.
- This is just an untested theory I am not sure what reality is actually like.
