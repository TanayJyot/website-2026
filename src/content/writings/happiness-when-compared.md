You watch someone on social media with a smiling face and you think that person is so much happier than you…

But there are a couple things to consider here

People try to portray their best selves on social media and your best self has been equally happy…

What makes some people happy may not make other people happy including you. You think that just because someone did this it will make me happy as well…

That’s when we forget that happiness isn’t about degree, it’s about frequency. How often you are happy matters more than how ‘well’ you are.

One simple question to ask yourself is would you be happy doing the same thing the same way? If the answer is no then you need to reconsider what makes you happy and focus on that instead.
