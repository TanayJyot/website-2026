---
title: "Life is shitty is just an excuse."
date: "2023-06"
label: "June 2023"
section: "short"
---

Life becomes shitty when you have to face the consequences of your actions but you did not learn enough from your past to be willing or yourself able to meet eye to eye with the repercussions. So instead of searching for a solution you decide to accuse life itself.

Make sure you build up today as such that you don't have to call your life shit tomorrow. That you have pre-made the solutions of the problems of your past so that when history repeats itself you have answers ready.
