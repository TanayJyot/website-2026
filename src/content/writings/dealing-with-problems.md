If you have problems then read books…

Because there may be a person who 150 years ago went through the exact problem and decided to study and write about it…

Because most of humanity’s problems don’t change with time. They just take a different shape…

And most straight to the point books are boring, but they are also the ones that give the best suggestions.

That is why they are not popular among the general crowd.

But to be extraordinary doing extraordinary things is part of the resume.
