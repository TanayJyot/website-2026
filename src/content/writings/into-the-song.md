When you are sitting in the music room, you realize some people have the right tempo but not the right scale they are over or under the appropriate scale for the song. Or they are missing a few notes here and there. Or they are missing the right tempo for the song making the song come too quickly or slowly.

But the most peculiar of them all is the person who has got all of this right, has everything under control but something seems off. Then you look at them and you realize they are not “into” the song. They are there singing but somehow the song isn’t running in their veins. They don’t feel what is actually being expressed in the song but why?

A song is an expression of feeling. Songs are used to pray to express feelings towards god. Songs are used as a source of emotions in movies. We use songs to relate to who we are based on our state of mind and what we are going through. They were also probably the first translation of stories into a different medium
