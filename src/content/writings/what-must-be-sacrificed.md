Doing a business is also about realising what must be sacrificed for what when it is sacrificed. For example, when Xiaomi entered the Indian consumer market, it was pretty clear, they couldn’t give software updates as good as Samsung or Apple but they still got the market because that time what mattered more was getting the consumer a cheaper product at the right time. A product with good hardware prominently in a price sensitive market. It is also an example of marketing without advertising. I also love the story of Manu Jain behind the company that even though he could get better jobs. He decided to work in a small office alone. The sacrifice that people make change the world and this is one such example. Manu Jain knew who and what needed him the most and he used that to guide him.

Recognising who and what needs you the most ;  he did and I can too!
