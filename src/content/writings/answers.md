date: "2024-03-14"
label: "March 14, 2024"

---

I want answers for questions that do not really have answers as if getting these answers will free me of all my worries or at least give me a definite reason to wake up everyday and act. I mean love for yourself might give you that drive or the act of doing something that serves the world but if everything is gonna kick itself anyways why even act

I am not saying that is an excuse to not act at all but my point is simply that acting does not make a lot of sense. It is not logical because there is sort of a missing component of truth into it. Why do something when you don’t really know where it is gonna lead. It is as if the system is designed on the basis of expectations and everything comes back to it being a game.

A game that does not really align with one thing or anything. There is a social aspect to that game and that is the only thing that makes sense. That you do things for those around you not for yourself but the problem is you don’t even know if they will be benefitted from it. You do have a defensive standpoint though that wherever this goes in the end you tried and just trying is not enough.

What people defend the argument with is that what can you even do about it, but not being able to do anything about it does not mean the problem is solved. Because that does not really do anything? But then what does anything? Maybe doing things for equity over doing things for efficiency.

It is like what is the point of being a type 2 or type 3 civilisation if everyone is just kicking themselves. Keep your goals in mind but reach them over a longer period of time. A time period where you can value the very same equity.

All my life I believed that all it is about is efficiency, the person who does the ‘most’ but that is the same as
> you won but at what cost

You want people to be happy? Then stop killing the heck out of them so that they can be more efficient because all that efficiency leads to is demise.

Loving people can kill that component of insecurity. When you don’t act out of ego but out of the love for doing the thing itself. Not because it is gonna change the rest of the save.

I used to believe that I am gonna destroy the world to save it but the world is self destructive in a bigger scale.
