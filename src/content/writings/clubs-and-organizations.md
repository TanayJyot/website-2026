running a large scale club is very different from running a large scale organization because you aren’t really optimizing for performance but you are really optimizing for development and growth of the people and it is kinda difficult to figure out what contributes to that and what does not and are you doing the right thing half the time because your outputs and your intentions are kinda disconnected.

It’s also really hard to transmit this message across people that hey it is not really about just doing the work, it’s about guiding people and helping them. It’s all literally just about helping the people and these things don’t need to be deeper than that so we need to stop treating them as if they are.
