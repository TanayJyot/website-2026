## Trying to sell it

I was around 14-15 probably younger when I went to sell a phone called the MI 5. That phone was from the company Xiaomi which had not become famous in India during that time so the phone was really niche and the back glass was broken. I wanted to sell the phone for 10000 rupees because I don’t remember why I wanted to buy a new one from what I can recall (or that was a different phone I was trying to sell I am not sure). I went to a place called Ravi Bhawan to do it. My grandfather was very sure they would not pay anything close to that in Ravi Bhawan. My dad was the one who gave me the idea to go to Ravi Bhawan though.

A place packed with phone shops with exorbitantly high rent prices. I went to every shop but I got rejected because I didn’t have the bill but I did have the box and the charger besides the phone was also niche. I was persistent regardless of all of that. I could only get one shopkeeper to even agree, and what he agreed on was out of sympathy because he also had the same phone with the back broken but he at least knew it was an amazing phone. He was offering around 5000 for the phone but I still went to another shop called Laxmi Telecom in another place called Katora Talab. Where he offered a price of around 3500, this guy had no sympathy because his shop was in fact really successful and even though he knew my dad, that did not change his attitude towards the business. I decided to agree with the guy offering me 5000 for the phone and called him where I think he told me he could not give me the money immediately so maybe in a couple days. However, I told my grandfather all of this and he ended up buying the phone from me.

My dad laughed when he heard my grandfather bought the phone.

## What I understood later

Now I realize why I was sent to places like this in the first place, it was always to learn what the real world is even about. My grandfather also bought it because I was his grandchild. My dad sent me there to understand that what I perceive as the value of something because it belongs to me does not mean everyone else will do so as well. What I noticed in myself here is that I persisted regardless. I still kept trying because that is when I saw that I wasn’t really scared of rejection. I was always a stoic to a lower extent but nothing like this broke me down. I sat in the office regardless of what happened because maybe out of everything I did see some utility of it. Maybe I complained so that I could get my mom’s attention that was taken away from me. The real world also cares a lot about self interest and my family, even though they left me so many times in these scenarios, when things got hopeless they were there behind my back. It was just so far away that  I couldn’t see it. The way it is now at my first semester at UofT. I love them for who they were regardless of whatever they did.

I also really believed in what I was selling was good because it actually was. If a goal was given to me I could work on it regardless of what it takes because I cared about it because of who it was given to me by.
