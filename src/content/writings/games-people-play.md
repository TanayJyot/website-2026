We are all just pawns of each other in an infinite game. If you believe yourself to be the player then you will be the one playing.

In this game, we are trying to reach some far away level. Thinking that the main story is all the matters.

What we forget is that the side quests are what prepare us for the main story. Even though reaching the main level is the objective, the side quests play an equally important role.
