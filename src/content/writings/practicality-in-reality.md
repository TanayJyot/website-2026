The kid that sits at home and studies all day may seem like the ideal kid for most parents. But it isn’t that simple…

This dream kid doesn’t know how to communicate with people or what clothes to wear, how to eat properly, how to be confident, how to negotiate…They aren’t presentable…

To survive in society, you need 10 other skills that can help you stand out. That can make you unique...

Although being at home will make you an amazing student, if you don’t know how to present yourself, it will be very difficult to stand up for yourself.
