---
title: "You deserve nothing"
date: "2023-07"
label: "July 2023"
section: "short"
---

The entire idea that I deserve something is flawed.

If you say you deserve something, you didn’t really do it for the sake of it but for your benefit. You can’t even ask for the best outcome in that case. You did a selfless act with selfish motives and then wonder why you didn’t get ‘what you deserve’.

Maybe you didn’t do what it takes. Just because you could handle a situation doesn’t mean you handled it well. When you would handle it well, you would know what you deserve because handling something well needs humility.
