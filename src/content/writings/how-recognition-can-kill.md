---
title: "How recognition can kill"
date: "2023-05"
label: "May 2023"
section: "short"
---

You think that when others recognise you for something then you have received a reward. You need this reward to feel that you have done anything.

But then people determine who you are because only then can you be rewarded.

This is how you lose your freedom and eventually your sense of self.

If you create content for Instagram and you get likes on certain type of content, you would wanna create more of that because you feel you have been rewarded. But that comes at a cost.

People need to feel that they are worth something but when they do nothing to justify their worth. They rely on other people to make them feel they are worth something. This is recognition.

Because you don’t see yourself as who you are and you don’t feel useful …
