There are people who finish a degree, follow that with a job which earns them between 8k to 10k an year, and feel they are really being underpaid for the value they provide. So they try to pay the government employees to get the government job but instead they get scammed and since they are poor no one gives more of a damn. As small of a deal this seems to be as I write it down. It isn’t, because this happens to so many people. These people dream of stability and all they can think about to get that is to get a government job. They try to do whatever they can to do so. Waste more money than what they gain trying to get themselves one, to watch their families be happy, suffer less.

They have the same sufferings we do. Similar family environments. Environments that make them want to escape their own families. We need to fix this, get people the lives they deserve that is proportional to the efforts they put in.

___

It is not about how this is a particular person’s case. This is the case of the village society. Those guys realise this and value education even less. This is the problem. Education itself is partially the problem but this is there too.
