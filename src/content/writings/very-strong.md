When you are very strong, you tend to think that nothing can overtake you. Even something like gambling and alcohol or even Valorant because you think that you have a powerful idea and it will prevent that from happening to you but the nuance is that you are delaying your idea via these things and they are just becoming a hindrance in your path and because you are so powerful. You are letting them be that hindrance. Knowing you strength of principles gives you a freedom to pull off things that are unacceptable because you know you can leave them by choice, even though they are killing you in the process as well and then it becomes a matter of recognising this.
