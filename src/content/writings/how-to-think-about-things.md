You choose to make yourself miserable everyday…

By thinking that other people could have done things differently and circumstances would have been different then your life would be better.

But circumstances or the behaviors are not in your control. So are you willing to trust chance with your life?

Because there is almost always something that you can do to change things as you want them to be rather than how they are.

We just think circumstances made us who we are as if we never made any conscious choices in our lives.
