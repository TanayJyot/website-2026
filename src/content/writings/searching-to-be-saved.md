[History keeps repeating itself](/writing/history-repeats)

Something that keeps repeating itself over and over again while we try to discover something completely new.

It is almost as if we are almost always searching for something to come and save us. For something or some moment or some time where we would make these changes. But as we grow up, it is almost as if this game entangles us more and more into itself.

There is no escape. You start to care so much about the responsibilities to the world that they are not as different from your own sense of self. This is why people associate with their job so much or who they think they are.
