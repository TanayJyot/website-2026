- Realise that  rich people suffer imagined troubles. What I mean here is people that engage in things that relate to addiction in a way are just harming themselves and then try to addict themselves in order to feel that they are being less harmed. They have troubles but you don’t want these kind of troubles in your life.
- Another thing that I think I seem to **envy** is the uniqueness and scarcity of what they have but that is the deal with it, they need to rely on these objects to fulfill their self worth another thing you would rather not have.
- The last thing is that fame comes at the cost of freedom a lot of the times. You need to be conscious of what you do at every moment if you are famous and that harms your authenticity. This is something I think kid Tanay suffered with. He could not be out of the ordinary without harming his self image. He needed other people to know who he was and that’s what stole a lot of his childhood away.
