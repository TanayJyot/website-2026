date: "2023-05"

How do you deal with the pain of past mistakes?

You simply outgrow them

You can either blame your past, stress over it and consider yourself incapable of being better…

Or you can consider yourself lucky, by ensuring that when history repeats itself, you will not fall for the same traps.

Or you can be inspired to make yourself better than what you used to be.
