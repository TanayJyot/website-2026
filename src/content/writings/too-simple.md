There are multiple ways to solve the same problem. Ways that are not restricted to a left and right solution. The ideology that going to a good college will get you a good life is too *simple* to be true. The same stands for that foreigners are only a certain way and only Indians value relationships.
>If something is too simple it’s probably not true

This is what I learnt from The Laws of Human Nature and Think Again. Things in real life aren’t that left and right. There are caveats and nuances. Associating certain qualities is in fact a sign of the attribution bias. I don’t want to attribute certain qualities without at least attempting to understand the person because then I am just closing my mind.
