Next time you find yourself comparing yourself to someone…

Think about the experiences you have had and would they have kept up in the same scenarios the way you did…

You compare yourself on the basis of something specific that you and someone else does and forget the rest… A specialisation

Your life’s experiences were designed to prepare you for something. Maybe they are better than you at something because that is where their priorities lie and that is where their experiences guide them.

Point being, our personality doesn’t lie in one thing. It is comprised of multiple combinators. When you compare yourself on the basis of a niche, you forget the gifts you have been handed.

Maybe they are good at this but from experience and persistence, I have learnt these 10 other things that if I combine with this niche I can become a more able person.

Specialisation matters but robots specialise better than us. It’s the 10 other things that we do that makes us unique and wanted. Embrace these things instead of overrating them.
