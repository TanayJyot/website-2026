---
title: "The role of the past"
date: "2023-06"
label: "June 2023"
section: "short"
---

Your past isn’t meant to hurt you or define you.

When things aren’t working out you can remember things from the past where you dealt with way harder things that did work out.

When things are going too well, the past can tell you to remember that everything can break apart in just yourself, get you back to the ground.

Use your past as a tool to give yourself humility, confidence and courage.
