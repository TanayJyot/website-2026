Experiences are like indications. You need to get something out of them. There maybe no pattern at all between experiences. But at least we need to explore the possibility that there may be one. It’s amazing how people value life so much. The lives of others around them more than anything. This bike guy crashing on my car was not a near death experience(It might have been in an alternate reality), but it did make me realise that I need to change the way I am doing things. I am not doing things to tick off boxes. I am doing things for the sake of them and because I wanna become a better person. The boxes are just reminders. For the past week, I have been ticking off boxes but I have also become more lenient of the things themselves. There are things to do and places to conquer. My not even real fear is restricting me. Even though I am learning stuff, I am loosening up on my own principles, because of my fears. The universe is giving me a lot to learn from but I have been ignoring all of it. But today is another day and that means there is the possibility that I can learn. This accident was a reminder of how limited my life is and how everything can be taken away in just a moment. How things could have been much more severe if the sequence of events would have been only a little different. I have this chance again and I need to ensure that this time it’s better than before.

You also decide that you don’t wanna do this and then you create excuses to justify yourself when there is no real reason to do so. There are things we can do if we just don’t tell ourselves here is the reason we can not do it. Man, there is so much. I need to get to the end of this, but the point isn’t reaching the end, it’s to explore the now.

You don’t win by putting others down, you just get yourself in deeper shit. Don’t try to be bigger on someone else’s expense Tanay because time will put you back in your place. Don’t expect reality to not bend back at you when you push towards it.
