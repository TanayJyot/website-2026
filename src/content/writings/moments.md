Life is lived in moments. Maybe it’s not a chain. Maybe it’s a sinusoidal graph composed of points. Points that don’t interlace. The problem arises when we try to act according to events as if there is a chain. We try to base all our actions on the basis of future events and by doing so we end up losing the moment. But soon Tanay you would learn, all that seem to matter are these very moments. Moments make you realise of the fascination that surrounds you. How everything lines up for things to happen. Maybe changing the world requires noticing the little subtleties of these moments and realising what they portray. Noticing not something away from you but something around you that no one else does. These moments do get you somewhere though.. Events do occur, things do change but that’s the beauty of events. There is an interconnection that surrounds me. Maybe there is a destiny. A sequence of events that will occur but I still believe I can manipulate destiny.
> The people who changed the world were the ones that believed they could.

([Changing the world](/writing/changing-the-world))
I need to believe I can manipulate destiny, change the sequence of events to do so. Only then can I put myself in a position wherein I do.
