Everyone says they want to improve but most people really don’t.

Because they do not want to change because change is difficult.

Because change is painful. It is easier to avoid pain and love pleasure. This very feeling binds all humanity.

We create the reason that we do not know what to do, but deep within ourselves we all know that one simple thing we can do to become better.

It is not always about how much work you do. It’s about putting some work in and putting a little more everyday. It isn’t tough to do but it is tough to be consistent.

The question is not even about what you need to do. It’s about what you need to be…

The kind of things you need to sacrifice, the people you will have to lose along the way and the systems you will need to build using pure will power. It is also about that.

What kind of person do you choose to be everyday?
