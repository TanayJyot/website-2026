title: "Stress in a manner is very exciting"
date: "2023-05-23"
label: "May 23, 2023"

---

I think stress in a manner is very exciting. Because when you are stressed you have a lot more things to do and you have responsibility and whatnot. All that is exciting as well and life feels bland without it. Sometimes what we consider as negative is an opportunity for us to surpass ourselves. To learn more about who we are and what we stand for and how when we will be in charge we will do things for the better. Tomorrow is gonna be decision day and I will accept whatever happens with all I got. Maybe you don’t do the best this time but fate taught you something recently. Changes in externalities won’t suddenly change your entire personality. Yes, they do affect you but it depends on how you process them. You won’t suddenly become a different person and forget all you are. It’s just like how the rich say that even if they start again, they are confident that they will be rich. Although, I do know that I will change my ways in order to do what it takes next time around. That is also the beauty of life. Even though it is so short and we must live with urgency because of that. Everyday is like a new life because we get to start again.
