My genuine desire at least I hope it is to take their conflicts from them make them my own and find a solution for them that keeps me going. At this point I don’t even understand why I am taking their problems and conflicts as if they were my own. I don’t know if I know how to resolve these things or how to deal with them but end of the day this does not make any sense to me mentally. Why am I so bothered by someone else’s problems when I am struggling to handle my own and deal with them on my own. It is almost ironic that after coming all the way here and dealing with so many different things to gain a stable mind. Someone that matters to me enough. Here or back home seems to bring me back to square one.

I hope in the future I reach a point where I can help all the good people in the world and the people that genuinely desire good for others to not suffer. I don’t really understand what that means.

The sad part is that you have to do the grieving for yourself and I cannot do much about it except keep offering you my hand. It is like the photo in Vatican city of god offering their hand and the person about to reach that hand but just falls short.

God will keep offering their hand, holding the hand and going with it is the choice of the person and that is what is the conflict here. There at least from my very very very limited understanding god does not feel a desire to give the hand. It is the duty of the god to do so.

The matter with me is that I have a desire to give the hand and every desire leads to unhappiness. [The Almanack of Naval Ravikant](/note/Jorgenson-The%20Almanack%20of%20Naval%20Ravikant). I do want to change it but is this my one big desire.

Well ig my one big desire is probably for people around me to do well and that desire is difficult to obtain unless I keep filtering people because that is the only way to ensure that they do well. Or I manipulate them which is even worse. I need to choose a desire that is based on what I do and not based on what everyone else does, because even though I have some influence on what everyone else does, I am not **them**. It is unfortunate based on my desire but it is the absolute truth. the truth is fundamental. How do I curb this desire into something that can achieve a similar goal
without the reliance on others?

My desire can be for me to do really really well so that when the people around me are actually suffering then I am in a position to give them my hand. If them coming to do well comes at the cost of my own well being then I will be sacrificing the self for the collective good. Because trying to help out one person would cause me to neglect the sufferings of all the others. I need to be in the right state to be able to do anything about anyone’s suffering and to be in the right state, I need to make sure I am well off and stable, at least mentally and that will happen when I do all my duties right.

Checking out is part of the duty, suffering for them is not.

___
It is interesting to think about where do you wanna go and choosing that path to decide how do you wanna proceed with things. Because tunnel visioning in that manner almost helps you resolve all of these other problems that you face.

So Tanay where do you wanna go?
