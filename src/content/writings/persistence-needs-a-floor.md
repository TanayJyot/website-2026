---
title: "Persistence needs a floor"
date: "2023-08-12"
label: "August 12, 2023"
section: "journal"
---

Today I was thinking about this idea that every person who is successful on planet Earth will try to say you need to keep going on and you need to keep doing what you are doing. persistence and everything. What I was doubting was the practicality of persistence. You still need a floor to fall into if everything falls apart. That is the thing, we all don’t see that floor while the rich ones do. Persistence and hard work still needs to be complemented by judgement. To decide when to do what. Maybe you don’t have money for your next business idea. But there are ways to get money, but before doing all of this you need to take a step back and think whether all of it is worth it in the first place. Is your idea really that good.
