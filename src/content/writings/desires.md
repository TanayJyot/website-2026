You can’t have everything in life…

But to try to do so we use fear. But after the fear is gone, we need that one desire that keeps us going it is about that desire.

So decide upon that one thing you want the most and put in everything to get that and ignore every other desire.

Because all of these other desires make no difference in your life but instead they just make you feel worse.

Your desire must give you a sense of purpose, direction and clarity. It should give you a reason to wake up everyday.

But when you desire to get the car your friend has and the body of your gym buddy and the mind of the class topper. You are really desiring nothing because your desires are no different from your distractions.

It’s about choosing what you wanna have the most and making it all that you desire and these petty things will not even cross your mind because you answer to a greater calling.
