- The Glottis is where the sound is produced as a big vibration and the vocal tract is everything after that would “shape” the sound which is why it makes sense to visualize the vocal tract as such

### Assumption of the model
- That the actual sound of a vowel is comprised of these 2 parts, so now we need to figure out how to separate these 2 from each other
- For this we use the [[cepstrum]]
- [[mel-frequency spectral coefficients]]
#### The "Stack"

If you were to trace the path of air:
1. **Lungs:** Provide the air pressure.
2. **Glottis (The Source):** The air hits the vocal folds in the neck and starts to vibrate.
3. **Vocal Tract (The Filter):** That vibration travels up through the throat and out the mouth, where the shape of your tongue and lips "filters" it into speech.
