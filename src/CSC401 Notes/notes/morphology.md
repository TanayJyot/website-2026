# morphology
##
> how words can be *changed* by [[inflection]]s or derivations
- how morphing a word changed its meaning

Morphology is ==the study of how words are structured, formed, and related to one another, focusing on the smallest units of meaning called morphemes== (e.g., roots, prefixes, suffixes). It analyzes how these components combine to form words and change their meaning or function, such as "cat" + "s" = "cats".
